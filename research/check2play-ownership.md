# Who is behind Check2Play — researched 3 August 2026

Question: does a larger holding company or an established review site stand
behind the Check2Play seal? Checked because `competitors.md` treats them as an
equally-credible incumbent alongside Gamecheck, and that equivalence has not
actually been verified.

## What their own site says

Legal entity, from the footer: **Trust Validator LTD, Independence Ave, P.1923,
Victoria, Mahé, Seychelles.**

Their "About" page: *"We are an independent verification platform... founded
with a simple yet crucial mission..."* — no named founder, no team page, no
history of the company, no mention of prior work or other ventures.

## What is findable outside their own site

**Nothing.** Three independent checks, all consistent:

1. **Search for founder/ownership** — turned up nothing about Check2Play beyond
   their own pages. The same search surfaced Gamecheck's founder by name (James
   Elliott) from a trade-press interview on the first try.
2. **Trade press** — Gamecheck has been covered by iGamingExpert, SlotBeats and
   iGamingBusiness, including a story about **partnering with Casino Guru** — a
   large, established review site — and a Brazil fake-games investigation picked
   up industry-wide. Check2Play has zero comparable coverage.
3. **Wayback Machine** — `gamecheck.com` has an archived snapshot. **`check2play.com`
   has never been archived at all**, at any point in its history. That is either
   a very young domain or one that has actively kept crawlers out — a page
   selling independent verification doing the latter would be a strange choice.
4. **"Trust Validator LTD"** returns zero results anywhere — no registry hit, no
   press mention, no LinkedIn page — despite Seychelles maintaining a public
   commercial register that surfaces unrelated shell-service companies easily in
   the same search.

## What this does and does not mean

**Does not mean:** that Check2Play is fake, a scam, or issues worthless seals.
None of this is proof of that, and it would be exactly the kind of unverified
accusation this project's own rules exist to prevent making about anyone else.

**Does mean:** Gamecheck and Check2Play should not be treated as equivalent
incumbents. One has a named founder, trade-press coverage, and a partnership
with an established review platform. The other has a Seychelles shell entity,
no independent coverage anywhere, and no web history prior to whenever it
appeared.

## Consequence for `competitors.md`

That document currently reads "two vendors independently at $250/month" and
"Check2Play already sells everything we planned to sell" as if both were
comparably credible market signals. They should not carry equal weight:

- Gamecheck's $250/month and scope is corroborated by outside coverage — a real
  market data point.
- Check2Play's page makes the same claims with no outside corroboration at all.
  It may be a genuine small operator with no press relationships, or it may be a
  newer entrant leaning on Gamecheck's category to look established. Either way,
  it should be cited as *one seal vendor's self-description*, not as confirmed
  market structure, until something independent of their own site backs it up.

This changes the confidence of one line in `competitors.md` — "there is no
product gap" rests partly on a vendor whose independent existence could not be
confirmed. It does not change the Gamecheck-based conclusions, which stand on
their own.
