# What we are actually offering casinos

Read this before any call or reply. Written 31 July 2026.

If you can only remember one sentence: **the first operators pay nothing, and
what they give us instead is a link and permission to say their name.**

---

## The offer in one paragraph

> Swift Secure independently checks a casino's licence against the regulator's
> own register, then checks that the games on the site come from the studios it
> claims. If it passes, the operator places a badge PNG on their site linking to
> their verification page on swiftsecure. That page is public, dated, and states
> exactly what was and was not checked. **For the founding operators this costs
> nothing** — no fee now, no card, no contract. In exchange we get a backlink
> and the right to name them as an early partner.

---

## Why we give it away first

This is the part to be clear-headed about, because it will come up internally.

A trust badge is worth exactly what its issuer's reputation is worth, and right
now ours is zero. Nobody has heard of Swift Secure. Charging €200/mo on day one
means selling an unknown seal to operators who have no reason to care — a very
hard sale that also produces no proof for the next conversation.

Giving the first cohort the badge free solves both problems at once:

- **It makes the price objection disappear.** "Free" removes the entire
  commercial negotiation from the first ten conversations. The only question
  left is whether they like the idea, which is the question we actually need
  answered.
- **It buys the asset we are short of: proof.** Ten live badges on ten real
  casinos is what makes conversation eleven a normal sale instead of a pitch.
- **The backlink has independent value.** Each partner links to us, which is
  what makes the directory rank at all. We are not working for free — we are
  being paid in the currency we need at this stage.

The honest framing internally: **we are not selling yet. We are buying
credibility, and the price is our own time.**

## What we must never say

The whole product is that our word means something. These are not style
preferences — break one and there is nothing left to sell.

- **Never call a casino "certified" or "verified" before the check has run.**
  The directory has a deliberate `listed` status meaning *in our index from
  public sources, no check performed*. It exists precisely so we never have to
  blur this.
- **Never imply a listed casino is endorsed.** Being in the directory is not a
  recommendation, and the pages say so.
- **Never claim a licence is valid because the casino says it is.** It counts
  when it is in the regulator's register, and not before.
- **Never invent a client count.** If asked how many operators we have, say the
  real number. "You would be among the first" is a *better* pitch to an early
  adopter than a vague implication of scale, and it has the advantage of being
  true.

The reason to be rigid here: our only differentiator against every other badge
in this market is that ours means something. Most do not. That is the whole
business.

## The three objections you will actually hear

**"Why should anyone trust *your* badge?"**
The strongest answer is the methodology page — specifically the part listing
what we do *not* check. Every competitor claims to check everything. Being
explicit about our limits is the only credible position available to a new
entrant, so lead with the limits rather than defending against them.

**"We already have a seal."**
Ask which one, then ask whether it links to a page showing when it was last
checked. Most do not — they are static images sold as a subscription with no
re-check. Ours is dated and re-verified. If theirs genuinely is better, note it
and move on; that is a real answer and there are 490 other prospects.

**"What's the catch if it's free?"**
Answer it straight: we need reference customers and backlinks, they need a trust
signal, and later cohorts will pay. Founding operators keep favourable terms.
Evasion here reads as a trap; candour reads as a startup, which is what we are.

## When to start charging

Not a decision for today, but the trigger is worth writing down now so it isn't
made emotionally later: **once operators start approaching us rather than the
reverse.** That is the signal that the badge has independent value. Until then,
the free cohort is doing exactly the job it was designed for.

The founding operators should stay free or heavily discounted permanently. It
costs little, and "we've been with them since the start" from a real operator is
worth more than their subscription.
