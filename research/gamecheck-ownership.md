# Who is behind Gamecheck — researched 3 August 2026

Follow-up to `check2play-ownership.md`. Having marked Check2Play's claims as
self-reported and unverified, the same scrutiny had to be applied to Gamecheck
before treating it as the confirmed baseline — praising one company's press
coverage without checking whether that coverage is itself independent is the
same mistake in a different shape.

## What their own site says

Legal entity, from the footer of every page: **Gamecheck Limited. Registered
Office: Madison Building Midtown, Queensway, Gibraltar GX11 1AA. Registered in
Gibraltar. Company number: 124930.**

No standalone "About" page (`/about` and `/company` both 404) — the company
information lives only in the footer, which is thinner than it first appeared.

## What is independently verifiable — and this time, it checks out

**1. A named founder, corroborated by outside interviews.** James Elliott is
named as founder/director across multiple trade-press pieces (iGamingExpert,
SlotBeats), and the Gamecheck homepage itself hosts an interview between him and
**Ashley Lang, CEO of Pragmatic Solutions** — a named executive at a real,
separate company, discussing the industry problem Gamecheck exists to solve.
That is a different kind of evidence than a company describing itself: a named
third party, at a named company, on the record.

**2. An ISO 27001 certificate from BSI Group, address-matched.** Found via
BSI's own public Client Directory (`bsigroup.com`), independent of any gaming
press:

- Registered address on the certificate: *Madison Building, Midtown Queensway,
  GX11 1AA, Gibraltar* — matches the website footer exactly
- Certificate IS 832990, scope covering "design, development, deployment and
  maintenance of the Gamecheck platform"
- Valid 27 Oct 2025 – 26 Oct 2028, currently active

BSI is a real, long-established certification body (the UK's national
standards body's commercial arm) unconnected to gaming or gambling. A company
that does not exist, or exists only as a marketing front, does not go through
an ISO 27001 audit — that process requires physical premises, documented
information-security controls, and an external auditor's sign-off.

**3. Wayback Machine history**, as noted in the earlier check.

## One loose thread, deliberately not pulled into the conclusion

A search for the company number also surfaced a **different, unrelated**
"Gamecheck Limited" — UK company number 03865462, incorporated 1999, registered
to a residential flat in London, status Active. Company House confirms this is
a separate legal entity from the Gibraltar one (different number, different
country, decades earlier incorporation, no stated connection). Almost certainly
an unrelated company that happens to share a generic name — "game check" is not
a distinctive enough phrase to assume affiliation from name alone. Noted so it
does not get mistaken for something suspicious later; it is not evidence of
anything about the Gibraltar entity.

## Conclusion: Gamecheck holds up under the same test Check2Play failed

| | Gamecheck | Check2Play |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Legal entity | Gibraltar, #124930, footer on every page | Seychelles shell, only on /about |
| Named person | Yes — founder interviewed by outside CEO | None anywhere |
| Independent certification | ISO 27001 via BSI, address-matched | None found |
| Trade press | Multiple outlets, unprompted coverage | Zero |
| Wayback Machine history | Present | Never archived |

**No holding company found — Gamecheck appears independently owned**, not a
subsidiary of a larger group or review platform. The Pragmatic Solutions
interview is a content collaboration between two separate companies, not
ownership. Nothing here points to Casino Guru, a review conglomerate, or any
parent entity holding Gamecheck.

This changes nothing about the risk `competitors.md` already records — the
category has a real, independently-verifiable incumbent at €250/month, which
is the fact that matters for pricing and for the "no product gap" conclusion.
What this check adds is confidence that the *evidence itself* is sound, not
just repeated from a page that could say anything about itself.
